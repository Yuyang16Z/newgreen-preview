Overview

Turnover boxes are the bloodstream of a factory: parts move in them, store in them, ship in them. These heavy-duty steel mesh boxes use rectangular welded frames with buckle-lock closures, built movable for constant circulation between stations, warehouses and suppliers.

Steel mesh means contents stay visible and drainable, and the boxes take forklift-and-floor abuse that breaks plastic totes. From just 2 pieces — outfit one cell before committing to a fleet.

Key features

  • Welded rectangular steel frames, mesh walls
  • Buckle-lock closures, movable design
  • Visible, drainable contents
