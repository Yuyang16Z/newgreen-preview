FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It depends on the product. Fabricated items like manhole covers start at just 2–10 pieces; standard hooks and brackets typically start at 100–1,000 pieces; shelving starts at 5 sets. The exact MOQ is listed on every product page.

Do you support OEM / ODM and custom fabrication?

Yes. We manufacture to your drawings or samples — custom dimensions, materials (Q235, 304/201 stainless, aluminum, Zn-Al-Mg alloy), surface finishes, logos and packaging. Stamping, laser cutting, bending, welding and tapping are done in-house.

Which payment terms do you accept?

T/T, L/C, D/P, D/A and Western Union. You can also place protected Trade Assurance orders through our Alibaba.com storefront.

Can I get samples before a bulk order?

In most cases yes — sample terms depend on the product and customization involved. Tell us what you need and we will confirm sample cost and time in our first reply.

What are your lead times?

Lead time depends on quantity and customization, so we quote it together with the price. Standard catalog items in regular quantities ship fastest; fully custom OEM parts need tooling time, which we state up front.

How do you ship international orders?

We ship worldwide from Ningbo — by sea for bulk orders, or by air and express for samples and urgent smaller lots. We can work with your forwarder or arrange the shipment for you.

Which markets do you currently export to?

Mainly Western, Northern and Eastern Europe, North America and Central America. Our products and documentation are prepared with EU and North American buyers in mind.

Are you a factory or a trading company?

Both, formally registered as manufacturer and trading company. Core products are made in our own Ningbo facility, which was verified through Alibaba.com's Onsite Check program.

Ready to compare quotes for your next order?

Send a product list, target quantities and destination port — we reply with pricing, lead time and shipping options within one business day.
