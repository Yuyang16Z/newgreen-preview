Privacy note

Short version: this website collects nothing.

This is a fully static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, embeds no third-party resources, and has no database.

The inquiry form does not transmit anything to this website — it opens a draft in your own email application, so your message travels as ordinary email from you to info@nbnewgreen.com. Emails you send us are used only to answer your inquiry and prepare quotations, and are never sold or shared.

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (IP address, requested URL, timestamp) for security and operations, as virtually all web hosts do.
