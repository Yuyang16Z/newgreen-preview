Overview

C-channel (C-type) steel is the workhorse section of ground-mount and industrial solar structures: strong in bending, easy to bolt, and adjustable anywhere along the channel. This line supplies adjustable C-channel brackets in hot-dip zinc or zinc-aluminum-magnesium coated steel.

Choose conventional hot-dip galvanizing for standard atmospheres or Zn-Al-Mg for coastal and high-humidity sites where cut-edge corrosion decides service life. Both run on the same channel dimensions, so the upgrade is a coating decision, not a redesign.

Key features

  • C-type steel channel, slide-in adjustability
  • Hot-dip zinc or Zn-Al-Mg coating options
  • Steel-section strength for demanding spans
  • Same dimensions across coating options
