Overview

An adjustable accessory line under our NG brand that bundles the hooks and brackets installers reach for most on pitched-roof photovoltaic jobs. Adjustability at the hook means one SKU covers a range of batten heights and tile profiles, simplifying purchasing and warehouse stock.

Like the rest of our roof-mounting range, these parts are produced in our Ningbo facility with in-house stamping and surface treatment, and can be mixed with our galvanized, Zn-Al-Mg and stainless hooks in one consolidated shipment.

Key features

  • Adjustable geometry covers multiple tile profiles
  • Reduces SKU count for distributors and installers
  • Combines with our full roof-hook range in one shipment
  • NG brand or OEM labeling
