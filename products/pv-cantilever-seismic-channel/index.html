Overview

Some mounting points can't be braced from both sides — that's cantilever territory — and some markets require seismic support engineering by code. This NG line supplies aluminum channel brackets for both duties: cantilevered panel supports and seismic bracing channels for PV systems.

Channel-based construction gives installers adjustment along the profile and a familiar strut-style assembly workflow. Sold factory-direct in project quantities.

Key features

  • Cantilever support for one-sided mounting points
  • Seismic bracing channels for code-driven markets
  • Strut-style channel assembly workflow
  • Factory-direct project pricing
