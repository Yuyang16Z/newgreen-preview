Overview

When plastic shelving sags and painted racks rust, stainless heavy-duty racking takes over. This MAYS line builds multi-level storage racks for commercial and industrial service — warehouses, basements, and cold storage where condensation kills ordinary steel.

Configurations run from back-of-house storage to full warehouse rows; stainless construction means the rack outlives the building fit-out with zero repainting.

  • Multi-level configurable storage
