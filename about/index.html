About Newgreen Technology

Ningbo Newgreen Technology Co., Ltd. is an Alibaba onsite-verified manufacturer and exporter of photovoltaic roof hooks, solar mounting systems, stainless-steel meat hooks, manhole covers and custom stamped metal parts — shipping factory-direct from Ningbo, China to Europe and the Americas.

Who we are

A young factory built for the solar decade

Ningbo Newgreen Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 2022 in Ningbo, Zhejiang — home to one of the world's busiest export ports and one of China's strongest metalworking regions. We are a manufacturer and trading company with a 51–100 person team and a production base of 5,000–10,000 square meters, generating an annual output value of US$5–10 million.

We built this factory for the solar decade. Our core lines serve the new-energy industry: photovoltaic roof hooks, tile-roof mounting structures, aluminum frames and rails, L-feet, carport and ground-mount systems, plus PV grounding and fastening hardware. Behind them runs the same precision metal shop that stamps automotive parts and makes our stainless-steel meat hooks for slaughterhouses and cold chains, fabricated manhole covers, storage shelving, and OEM stamping, laser cutting and machining work.

Being young is our edge, not our excuse: the equipment is modern, the engineering team answers fast, and every order still gets owner-level attention. We buy raw material from publicly listed corporations, run production under an ISO 9001 quality management system with 6S shop-floor discipline, and apply a double-inspection standard before anything ships. Products go out under our MAYS and NG brands or under yours, to Western, Northern and Eastern Europe, North America, Central America and Southeast Asia — with flexible payment terms and Trade Assurance available through our Alibaba.com storefront.

Quality & compliance

Engineered, inspected and shipped to spec

  • Raw material from listed corporations — Q235 carbon steel, 304/201 stainless, aluminum and Zn-Al-Mg alloys purchased from publicly listed mills and matched to each order's specification.
  • ISO 9001 system, double inspection — Manufacturing runs under an ISO 9001 quality management system with 6S shop-floor management and a double-inspection standard from incoming material to pre-shipment.
  • Corrosion protection built in — Hot-dip galvanizing, anodizing, polishing and shot-blasted finishes for decades of outdoor and food-contact service.
  • Independently verified — Alibaba.com Onsite Check: our legal status and manufacturing premises were confirmed by a third-party verification company.
