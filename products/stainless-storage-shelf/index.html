Overview

Freestanding multi-layer shelving built from stainless steel: 304 for wet, food-contact and corrosive settings, or 201 as the economical choice for dry storage. Shelf heights adjust so the same unit stores stockpots today and sheet pans tomorrow.

Stainless shelving is standard equipment in commercial kitchens, cold rooms, laboratories and pharmacies because it doesn't rust, doesn't harbor odors, and wipes truly clean. Sold in sets, from 5 sets per order.
