Overview

Model NG 260526-6 uses zinc-aluminum-magnesium coated steel to combine the strength of a steel section with corrosion protection that goes beyond conventional galvanizing: the Zn-Al-Mg layer forms protective products that migrate over cut edges, drill points and scratches.

The result is a high-strength PV panel support that holds its integrity in coastal and industrial atmospheres — the environments that age ordinary coated brackets fastest — while staying cost-competitive against stainless alternatives.

Key features

  • High-strength steel section for demanding spans
  • Zn-Al-Mg coating with cut-edge self-protection
  • Strong fit for coastal and industrial atmospheres
  • Cost-effective alternative to stainless steel
