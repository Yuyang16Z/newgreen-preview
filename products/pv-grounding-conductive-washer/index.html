Overview

Anodized aluminum doesn't conduct — which is why PV arrays need piercing hardware. These MAYS conductive sheets and double-spike piercing gaskets bite through the anodized layer of panel frames and rails as the clamp torques down, creating the electrical bond that grounding and lightning-protection systems depend on.

Stamped from stainless steel for corrosion-stable conductivity over the array's life, they are a tiny line item that inspection failures make expensive to forget. OEM versions to match your clamp geometry are available.

Key features

  • Double-spike design pierces anodized coatings
  • Creates panel-to-rail grounding continuity
  • Supports lightning-protection bonding paths
  • Stainless steel keeps contact stable for decades
  • OEM geometries for specific clamp systems
